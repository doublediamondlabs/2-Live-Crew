# Move your existing race records

1. On the original app/device, use **Setup → Export All Races**.
2. Keep that JSON outside your phone, such as a private drive folder.
3. Publish this portable app and open its new address on the same phone.
4. In the new app, use **Setup → Restore Backup & Printing → Restore a Backup**.
5. Choose the JSON. Restoration replaces all races at the new address; export anything there first.
6. Verify race names, runners, recent check-ins, recipes, gear, and keepsake information.
7. Install the new address as a separate phone app, then test an airplane-mode close/reopen.
8. Keep the original app and backup until migration and offline tests pass.

The two apps remain separate copies. They do not synchronize. A backup does not move automatically when you update or change a hosting URL. Export from whichever copy holds the newest records.

To share the app, share its published web address. Do not upload your personal race-backup JSON to the public repository. To hand your records to another crew member, transfer the JSON privately and restore it on their device.
